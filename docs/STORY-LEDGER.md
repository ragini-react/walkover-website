# Our Story: evidence ledger

This ledger records the evidence behind every historical claim on `/our-story`. The page content lives in `src/content/story.ts`, and each source ID below matches a `sources` entry there.

Research was done on 10 October 2026. Quotes were copied from the full text of each page, downloaded directly or from the Internet Archive where the live site blocks automated access (thegritstories.com and yourstory.com return 403).

**Status key**

- **Verified.** A primary or contemporary document says it: a regulator notice, an archived page from the time, or a press release dated at the time.
- **Reported.** A founder, the company or a journalist says it, and nothing independent confirms it. The page attributes it in the text.
- **Inferred.** Our interpretation. The page only uses it inside "Our reading" boxes.
- **Unverified / Contradicted.** Not stated as fact on the page.
